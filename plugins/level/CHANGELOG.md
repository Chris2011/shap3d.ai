# Changelog – Level editor plugin

## [1.0.0] – Shap3D.ai (unreleased)

### Added

- A plugin package of its own (`plugins/level`, built with `vite build`, installed via _Plugins › Load plugin folder…_), with its own lint, format, type and text checks.
- Blockout: rooms, corridors (bending once) and stairs on a grid – also on floors above each other (a gallery over a hall, stairs up into it), drawn with the block tool (key K) or the Blockout panel, built into one group with floors, walls and optional ceilings – corridors open into rooms at their ends, rooms side by side share one wall, level doors cut doorways (and snap onto the walls' lines); the pieces listed to change or remove; `s3d.levelBlock()`, `s3d.levelBlockEdit()` and `s3d.levelBlockout()`.
- Level objects as components of the model, in four groups – players and enemies (spawn, enemy, waypoint), volumes scaled with the Scale tool (trigger, hazard, sound zone), items and doors (pickup, door with lock and key, save point), cameras and movement (fixed camera with its view drawn, jump pad, teleporter) – each placed object with its own id and properties as plugin data on the instance.
- Actions of triggers and pickups on other objects: open, close, unlock and lock doors, switch objects on and off, let enemies come, switch the fixed camera, teleport the player, play a sound, show a message – drawn as dashed links; changing an id changes what points at it.
- A place tool (key J): the chosen kind follows the pointer on the surface under it, a click places it, Shift places more, R turns by 45°.
- The Level panel: the kinds by group, name, id, properties (texts, numbers, switches, choices, targets) and actions of the selected object, the list of all objects by kind, export.
- Light and atmosphere: light objects (all around or spot, colour, brightness, reach, cone, on or off, flickering, casting shadows – switched by triggers) and the level's mood (daylight, fog with colour and reach), shown while editing when wanted and always in the test run, with the player's torch (F, casting shadows); `s3d.levelAtmosphere()`; the atmosphere in the level file.
- A test run (key P, the Level panel, commands for first person and from behind): walking with collision against the model, stairs, jumps and gravity; first person (mouse look with a locked pointer), from behind, and through the fixed cameras switched by triggers; triggers, doors with keys, pickups, teleporters, jump pads, hazards and save points work; sounds play – sound zones loop theirs with the echo of a room, hall or cave, _Play sound_ plays one once – from the user's own files (Level panel › Sounds) or a stand-in made up from the name; enemies walk – they patrol their waypoints (waiting where a waypoint says), see the player ahead of them and not through walls, follow, give up after a while and hurt on contact, each of a count on its own; a display with health, inventory, messages and controls; the model is left as it was.
- What can be reached: a walking grid over the model's floors (several above each other, headroom, away from walls, low steps, teleporters and jump pads) from the player's spawn points, shown in the view (green reached, orange not) and following changes; pickups, doors, save points, triggers, teleporters and jump pads that cannot be reached and spawn points without a floor are listed; a command and `s3d.levelNav()`; the level file carries the grid (`nav`: points and links in glTF's space).
- Checks, listed in the panel and the level file: no spawn point, the same name or id twice, actions and teleporters pointing at nothing, locked doors whose key nothing gives, no first camera, enemies that never come.
- A level mode next to the model in the top bar: only the level tools in the toolbar and the level panels on the right, names above the objects.
- A floor plan generator: rooms of random size linked by corridors with loops from a seed, as the blockout, with game objects when wanted (start, lights, enemies, health, save point, goal); in the Blockout panel and as `s3d.levelGenerate()`.
- Engine templates for Godot 4 (GDScript EditorScript), Unity (`Shap3dLevelObject.cs` with an import menu) and Unreal (Python editor script) that build the level's game objects next to the model's GLB; `s3d.levelTemplate()`; the level file also carries each object's place in the model's own space (`model`: position, yaw, pitch).
- Export: the level file (JSON) with id, kind, name, glTF node, position, rotation, scale, size, properties, actions and a camera's view of every object in glTF's space – next to the app's glTF export; copies get ids of their own first.
- The data format `level.object` (version 1) and the script functions `s3d.levelObject()`, `s3d.levelObjects()` and `s3d.levelFile()`.
- Texts in English, German, French, Spanish and Arabic.
