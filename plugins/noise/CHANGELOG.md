# Changelog – Noise plugin

## [1.0.0] – 2026-10-27 · Shap3D.ai (unreleased)

### Added

- A plugin package of its own (`plugins/noise`, built with `vite build`, installed via _Plugins › Load plugin folder…_), with its own lint, format, type and text checks.
- Seeded simplex noise in 2D and 3D with fBm, ridged multifractal and billow, domain warping and terraces.
- Noise terrains with a live preview: size, detail, seed, scale, height, octaves, persistence, lacunarity, valleys ↔ peaks, warp, island falloff, terraces, water and snow; five presets (hills, mountains, island, canyon, dunes). They keep their settings (select one to change it in place) and their height grid in the built-in terrain plugin's format, so the Terrain tool sculpts them further.
- Trees grown from seeds: oak, birch, pine and palm – branching trunks bent by noise, leaf clusters, whorls and fronds; height, gnarl and crown density; also as kinds to scatter.
- Rivers dug through a terrain's valleys (meandering, a bed that only falls, wider downstream, banks, water) and roads or gravel paths that find the gentlest way below a steepest grade – cut and filled into the terrain, wide bends, bridges over rivers and deep hollows, a dashed centre line; along selected edges too. Roads meet the roads built before in junctions: T-junctions ending at the other road's edge, crossings split into two pieces, roads going on from another road's end, corners rounded off, heights matched; a road to or from another road's end comes in straight; a city's streets count as roads, so later roads go on from their ends or cross them. Found routes keep off the terrain's border, and hairpins tighter than the road is wide turn around one point on the inside instead of folding over. In the tab _Nature_ and as `s3d.river()` / `s3d.road()`.
- Villages on a terrain (`s3d.villages()`, tab _City_): on gentle sites, a main street along the contour through the centre with side streets branching off it, houses in rows beside them on plinths with gable or hip roofs, connected by roads that find the gentlest way around the other villages.
- Scattering on a terrain leaves the cities, villages, rivers and roads built on it free.
- Hydraulic erosion (raindrops dig gullies and fill valleys, a thermal pass for steep slopes) – as a terrain setting with preview, as `s3d.erode()` and in the context menu for any terrain.
- Scattering conifers, broadleaf trees, bushes, rocks or the model's own components on a terrain by a noise mask – spacing, density, clumping, slope and height limits, random size and turn.
- Scattering along selected edges (a path – beside it on alternating sides and facing along it, e.g. an avenue) and on selected faces (optionally standing along the face normal), besides terrains.
- Stones from cellular (Voronoi) noise: flagstones, cobbles, slabs and dry walls as real stones with joints, chamfered tops, varied heights and shades and optional mortar – on selected faces (floors and walls) or an area; presets and a tab of their own.
- Facades: windows with frames and sills, shutters, stacked balconies, cornices and a ground floor with shop windows and a door, on selected wall faces or all walls of a group, varied per window by the seed.
- Cities: streets, pavements, blocks split into plots and a building on each whose height follows a noise density map (high centre, low edges), with setbacks and roof boxes, gable and hip roofs on low buildings, perimeter blocks round courtyards, parks, street trees and road markings (centre lines, zebra crossings); on a selected terrain (`s3d.city({ on })`) as a hill town – eased slopes, every block a level terrace on retaining walls, streets following the ground; a tab _City_ for facades and cities.
- When the Terrain plugin keeps its height grid in another version of the format, the plugin still starts and says so – terrains of that version are neither read nor changed.
- Deforming groups: refined without cracks, twisted, tapered and bent along their height and roughened by 3D noise (moved along the normals); terrains get the noise on their height grid.
- Three material shaders: _Noise_ (two colours mixed by fractal noise), _Cells_ (a Voronoi pattern with joints and a shade per cell) and _Facade_ (windows in a grid of storeys, lit or dark, with a shop floor).
- Script functions `s3d.noise`, `s3d.fbm`, `s3d.cellNoise`, `s3d.noiseTerrain`, `s3d.scatter`, `s3d.tree`, `s3d.erode`, `s3d.river`, `s3d.road`, `s3d.villages`, `s3d.paving`, `s3d.facade`, `s3d.city` and `s3d.noiseDeform`, an example for the script editor, commands per tab and context menu entries.
- Building steps: everything built on a terrain (erosion, rivers, roads, cities, villages, scattering) is kept as a step – switch it off, reseed or remove it and the terrain is built again; new settings for a noise terrain build its steps again on the new heights; sculpting with the Terrain tool becomes a step of its own right away (in the same undo step). The height grid is read and written through the app's data format `terrain.grid`. In the tab _Terrain_ and as `s3d.terrainSteps()`, `s3d.editTerrainStep()` and `s3d.rebuildTerrain()`.
- The generators live in the library `packages/procgen` (with tests) and are bundled into `plugin.js` – no other plugin is needed at runtime.
- Eleven screenshots with their demo scripts (`npm run screenshots`).
- Texts in English, German, French, Spanish and Arabic.
