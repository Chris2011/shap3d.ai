# Changelog – Languages

## [1.0.4] – Unreleased

### Added

- German, French, Spanish and Arabic texts for ambient occlusion in the Styles panel for the rotation grips of the Move tool (tooltip and status line), for switching snapping on and off, for moving with the arrow keys for rounding corners with the 2-point arc for rectangles aligned with an edge for leaning against a face when rotating and for moving from an outline edge.

## [1.0.3] – 2026-10-25 · Shap3D.ai 0.10.0

### Added

- German, French, Spanish and Arabic text for the switch between the model and the workspaces of plugins in the top bar.
- German, French, Spanish and Arabic texts for shader materials in the Materials panel and in scripts.
- German, French, Spanish and Arabic texts for own image textures in the Materials panel (image, width, height, offset, rotation).
- German, French, Spanish and Arabic texts for plugin bundles in the plugin manager and for the messages of plugin data formats.

## [1.0.2] – 2026-10-25 · Shap3D.ai 0.9.0

### Added

- German, French, Spanish and Arabic texts for the tape measure's guide point mode (status line and instructor), the scene tabs, the outliner sorted by tags, _To scenes…_ in the Tags panel and the purge reminder when saving.

### Fixed

- The template for new languages (`template.json` and the editor's _Template_ button) only holds the texts of the core and of this plugin – other plugins, built-in or not, bring their own language files.

## [1.0.1] – 2026-10-22 · Shap3D.ai 0.7.0

### Added

- Translations for the new switches of the ground grid (axes, labels) in German, French, Spanish and Arabic.

## [1.0.0] – 2026-10-20 · Shap3D.ai 0.6.0

### Added

- German, French, Spanish and Arabic (right-to-left) for the app; English is built in and the fallback.
- The language switch in the top bar and a _Language: …_ command per language.
- The translation editor: areas, search, progress, live preview and _where is this text?_, import and export of language files (JSON) and a template for new languages.
