# Changelog – Import formats

## [1.2.0] – Unreleased

### Added

- Export as **USDZ** (Apple AR, Pixar USD).
- Export of the **view as SVG**: the visible edges and outlines of the 3D view as vector lines, with a choice of width.

## [1.1.0] – 2026-10-20 · Shap3D.ai 0.6.0

### Added

- All texts in English, German, French, Spanish and Arabic.

## [1.0.0] – 2026-10-19 · Shap3D.ai 0.5.0

### Added

- Import of FBX, Collada, KMZ, 3DS, PLY, 3MF, AMF, VRML, USD/USDZ and Blender files as a built-in plugin. The three.js loaders and the Blender reader load only on the first import; the app turns the scene into faces, guesses the units and places it.
